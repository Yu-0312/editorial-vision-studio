# Period Travel Poster — Style DNA

| Dimension | Level |
|-----------|-------|
| Typography | ★★★★ |
| Geometry | ★★★★ |
| Negative Space | ★★★ |
| Texture | ★★★ |
| Color | ★★★★★ |

## Characteristics

- Mid-century travel and exhibition poster language: lithograph and screenprint lineage
- Saturated flat colour fields — the ground is ink, not paper
- Landscape or landmark reduced to bold stacked shapes, hard edges, no gradient modelling
- Strong horizon or diagonal; deliberate low sun / long shadow geometry
- Destination or event name set large as part of the composition, not captioned onto it

## Typography

Condensed or geometric sans as a structural block — Futura, Gill Sans, condensed grotesk. Wordmark scale, letterspaced. One secondary line maximum.

## Palette

Four to six flat saturated inks: deep teal, burnt orange, ochre, cream, ink navy, tomato red. Chosen as an ink set, not extracted verbatim from a photo.

## Reject if

- Photographic surface detail, airbrush gradients, or depth-of-field blur
- More than six colours, or any colour that is not a flat area
- Riso grain, halftone dots, or scan defects (PRINT tier is `zine` only — see [../assets/texture.md](../assets/texture.md))

## Compiler clauses

```
Period travel poster, saturated flat ink ground, landscape reduced to bold
stacked flat colour shapes with hard edges and no gradient modelling, strong
horizon or diagonal, condensed geometric sans destination name set large as
part of the composition, four to six flat inks, matte poster stock.
```

## Mood

optimistic, graphic, mid-century
