# MUJI Editorial — Style DNA

| Dimension | Level |
|-----------|-------|
| Typography | ★★ |
| Geometry | ★★ |
| Negative Space | ★★★★★ |
| Texture | ★★★ |
| Color | ★★ |
| Decoration | ★ |

## Characteristics

- Extreme simplicity, empty space
- Natural materials, functional calm
- No headline drama, no complex geometry

## Palette

white, wood tone, gray, earth tone

## Reject if

- Huge headline, many graphics, complex geometry, saturated accents

## Compiler clauses

```
Extreme negative space, white and warm gray palette, no decorative type,
single small caption, functional calm composition.
```

Append `natural material texture` **only** when `design_tokens.texture_tier` resolves to SURFACE. On a FLAT target the clause is dropped, not softened — see [../assets/texture.md](../assets/texture.md).

## Mood

minimal, peaceful, functional
